FAQ

Common questions

Do you guarantee I'll rank higher on Google?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings; Google decides those. What we do is fix the things in your control that commonly hold local businesses back, and give you tools to keep improving.

Do I need to give you my passwords?

No. You add us as a manager on your Google Business Profile, and you can remove us anytime. For website fixes, we'll work with whoever manages your site, or use a limited access login you create.

Who owns everything when you're done?

You do. Your profile, photos, posts, review materials and website remain yours.

Is there a contract?

A simple signed service agreement, no long-term contract. The Fix Sprint is a one-time project paid upfront. Monthly Care is optional: cancel anytime; it renews monthly until canceled, and each monthly payment is covered by the 30-day, 100% money-back guarantee.

How long does it take?

5 business days. Day 0 is when work begins, once we have your access and your answers to a short intake questionnaire. If we need to create a new Google listing for you, Google's verification can take extra time beyond the 5 business days; that part is up to Google.

What if I don't have a Google Business Profile yet?

Creating one is included in the sprint. We set up a new Google Business Profile in your own Google account, so you own it. Google requires the business to be verified, and that can take extra time beyond the 5 business days. We'll walk you through it.

What if I don't have a website?

That's fine. The sprint focuses on your Google profile. The website quick fixes apply only if you already have a site.

What's your refund policy?

30-day, 100% money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied for any reason, request a refund within 30 days of payment and get 100% of your money back. After 30 days, payments are final. Refunds may be refused only in cases of fraud or abuse. Refunds go back to your original card. Read the full refund policy.

Who do you work with?

Local businesses in Pleasanton, Jourdanton and across Atascosa County and the greater San Antonio area. Much of the work can be done remotely, so nearby areas are welcome too.
