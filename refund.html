Refund Policy

For services provided by J&L Professional Services, a J&L Holdings company.

Draft — review before publishing. This policy is a plain-language starting point, not legal advice. Confirm it matches how you actually handle refunds.

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy applies to services sold by J&L Professional Services, including the Local Visibility Fix Sprint and the Monthly Care plan. Products from other J&L Holdings businesses (such as AGAPE or AGAPE Digital) will have their own policies when they launch.

Local Visibility Fix Sprint (paid upfront)

Before work starts: full refund

If you cancel before we begin work on your sprint, you will receive a full refund. Work begins when we start making changes or preparing deliverables after receiving your access and intake details; we will tell you when your sprint has started.

After work starts, before delivery: prorated refund

If you cancel after work has started but before we deliver your before/after report, we will refund the portion of the price for work not yet completed. Anything already completed is yours to keep.

After delivery: no refund

Once the sprint is delivered, which is marked by sending your before/after report, the service is complete and is not refundable. If something we agreed to do is missing or wrong, tell us within 14 days and we will fix it at no charge.

No ranking guarantee

Google controls search rankings. We do not guarantee any specific ranking, traffic or number of calls, and refunds are not issued based on ranking results.

Delays outside our control

The 7-day timeline starts once we have the access and information we need. Delays caused by waiting on access, approvals or Google verification do not qualify for a refund, but we will always keep you informed.

Monthly Care ($99/month)

Monthly Care is month-to-month with no contract. You can cancel anytime before your next billing date and you will not be charged again. Months already billed are not refunded, though service continues through the end of that month.

How to request a refund or cancel

Email jeromey.littlejohn@yahoo.com or call (210) 417-2771. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.
