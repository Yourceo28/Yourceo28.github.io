Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy applies to services sold by Fix Sprint (owned and operated by Jeromey Littlejohn), including the Local Visibility Fix Sprint and Monthly Care. Fix Sprint is separate from J&L Holdings; J&L Holdings businesses (such as AGAPE or AGAPE Digital) will have their own policies when they launch.

30-day, 100% money-back guarantee

If you're not satisfied for any reason, request a refund within 30 days of payment and get 100% of your money back. After 30 days, payments are final. Refunds may be refused only in cases of fraud or abuse.

This applies to the Local Visibility Fix Sprint ($297, paid upfront) and to each Monthly Care payment.

No ranking guarantee

Google controls search rankings. We do not guarantee any specific ranking, traffic or number of calls. The 30-day money-back guarantee still applies.

Delays outside our control

The 5-business-day timeline starts at Day 0, once we have the access and information we need. If we create a new Google Business Profile for you (in your own Google account, which you own), Google's verification can take extra time beyond the 5 business days. We will always keep you informed.

Monthly Care ($99/month)

Monthly Care is month-to-month: cancel anytime. It renews automatically each month until you cancel. You can cancel by email (jeromey.littlejohn@yahoo.com) or phone ((210) 417-2771); cancel before your next billing date and you will not be charged again. Each monthly payment is also covered by the 30-day, 100% money-back guarantee.

How to request a refund or cancel

Email jeromey.littlejohn@yahoo.com or call (210) 417-2771. Approved refunds go back to the original card used to pay, typically within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.
