Refund Policy

For services provided by J&L Professional Services, a J&L Holdings brand, owned and operated by Jeromey Littlejohn.

Draft — review before publishing. This policy is a plain-language starting point, not legal advice. Confirm it matches how you actually handle refunds.

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy applies to services sold by J&L Professional Services, including the Local Visibility Fix Sprint and Monthly Care. Products from other J&L Holdings businesses (such as AGAPE or AGAPE Digital) will have their own policies when they launch.

Local Visibility Fix Sprint (paid upfront)

Before work starts: 100% refund

If you cancel before we begin work on your sprint, you will receive a full (100%) refund. Day 0 is the day work begins, after we receive your access and intake details; we will tell you in writing when Day 0 starts.

Days 0–2: 50% refund

If you cancel on Day 0, Day 1 or Day 2 of your sprint, you will receive a 50% refund. Anything already completed is yours to keep.

Day 3 on: no refund

From Day 3 of your sprint on, including after delivery, the sprint is not refundable.

14-day fix window after delivery

Delivery is marked by sending your before/after report. If something we agreed to do is missing or wrong, tell us within 14 days after delivery and we will fix it at no charge.

No ranking guarantee

Google controls search rankings. We do not guarantee any specific ranking, traffic or number of calls, and refunds are not issued based on ranking results.

Delays outside our control

The 5-business-day timeline starts at Day 0, once we have the access and information we need. If we create a new Google Business Profile for you (in your own Google account, which you own), Google's verification can take extra time beyond the 5 business days. Delays caused by waiting on access, approvals or Google verification do not qualify for a refund, but we will always keep you informed.

Monthly Care ($99/month)

Monthly Care is month-to-month with no contract, and it renews automatically each month until you cancel. You can cancel anytime by email (jeromey.littlejohn@yahoo.com) or phone ((210) 417-2771); cancel before your next billing date and you will not be charged again. Months already billed are not refunded, though service continues through the end of that month.

How to request a refund or cancel

Email jeromey.littlejohn@yahoo.com or call (210) 417-2771. Approved refunds go back to the original card used to pay, typically within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.
