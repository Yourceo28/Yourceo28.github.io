Approach

How we operate

A few plain principles guide every business we start or own.

Build lean

We start small, keep overhead low, and grow a business only after it proves that customers want what it offers.

Stay disciplined

Each venture has a clear focus, honest pricing and simple terms. We would rather do fewer things well than many things poorly.

Own for the long term

We build companies to keep and operate, not to flip. Decisions are made with years in mind, not quarters.

Serve people well

Our work is grounded in faith and integrity. We treat customers, partners and our community the way we would want to be treated.
