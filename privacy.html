Privacy Policy

How J&L Holdings and its businesses handle your information.

Draft — review before publishing. This policy is a plain-language starting point, not legal advice. Have it reviewed and update it if your practices change.

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how J&L Holdings and its businesses, including J&L Professional Services ("we," "us"), collect, use and protect information when you visit this website or work with us.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us: your name, email, phone number, business name and anything you include in a message, email or call.
  • Client project information: when you hire us, the business details needed to do the work, such as your business name, address, hours, services and photos, and the manager access you grant to your Google Business Profile.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by a third-party payment processor (such as Stripe). We do not see or store your full card number.
  • Basic technical data: our website host may automatically log standard information like IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and performance.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and provide the services you request.
  • To send invoices, receipts and project updates.
  • To improve our website and services.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Contact form

The contact form on this site opens your own email app with your message pre-filled. Your message is sent by your email provider, and we receive it as a normal email.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business (for example, payment processing, email and web hosting), when you ask us to (for example, publishing approved updates to your Google profile), or when required by law.

Access to your accounts

We do not ask for your passwords. Any manager access you grant us can be removed by you at any time, and we will remove our own access on request or when a project ends if you prefer.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy.

Data retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we take reasonable steps to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us below.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows the latest version.
